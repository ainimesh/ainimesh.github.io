    EquiSite

    Equivariant graph neural networks for end-to-end prediction of multiple ligand binding sites

    EquiSite predicts every ligand binding site of a protein as a set, without clustering, learned queries or Hungarian matching. Each residue scores itself as a possible site anchor, and each selected anchor draws its own site mask. The page lets you run the decode on real model outputs and rotate the predicted sites in 3-D.
